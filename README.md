# Sika
Sika
Where a chat becomes a deal.
Sika is a private messenger for family, friends, work and the market, built in Nigeria for Africa and the world. Messages, files and calls are encrypted on your phone, and the servers that carry them cannot read them. When a conversation turns into a sale, a buyer and a seller can agree a price, send an order card and hold the payment in escrow until the goods arrive, all inside the same chat.
Website and download: https://sikaos.app
Status: early access for Android. This repository holds the public story of the project. The source code is private for now.
What works today
One to one chats and groups of up to 256 people, with roles, announcements, polls, mentions, and invite links with QR codes
Photos, documents, videos and voice notes, with a preview before you download
Replies, reactions, editing, forwarding, disappearing messages and read receipts
Statuses that last 24 hours, with reactions and replies
Voice calls, video calls, and group calls for up to four people
Orders and escrow steps inside the chat, with a card that both people see
A market for what people near you are selling, and public channels you can follow
English, French, Kiswahili, Hausa, and Arabic (with right-to-left layout)
A PIN lock, safety numbers to verify a contact, and an encrypted backup you can restore on a new phone
